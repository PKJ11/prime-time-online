      How to play

      1. Who starts? Everyone gets a card. The smallest number starts and gets the 1 card.
      2. First move. Put the 1 on the board.
      3. Your turn. Play one card. To play a card, tap it twice. If a card fits, you must play it. If nothing fits, tap Pass.
        • A prime number fits any time its slot is free.
        • Other numbers need their prime factors on the board first. 12 needs 2, 2 and 3. =××
        • MAGIC is any prime. Put it on an empty prime slot. When all prime slots are full, it can go on top of a card.
        • SKIP: the player you pick misses a turn. They still take a card.
      4. Always take a card from the box after your turn.
      5. Your cards stay in the order you got them. Hold a card and drag it to move it.
      6. Winning. When the box is empty, the first player with no cards wins.
      7. Scores. Everyone else adds up their cards. MAGIC and SKIP are 0. The lowest score is best.
      8. Small screen? Tap the board to see it bigger. Tap again to go back.
