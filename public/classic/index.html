          How to play Prime Time

          1. The toss. Everyone gets a random card (2–60). Smallest number wins the toss and becomes P1. Ties draw again.
          2. The deal. P1 gets the 1 card plus 4 random cards. Everyone else gets 5 random cards.
          3. First move. P1 must place the 1 on the board. Play then goes clockwise.
          4. On your turn place one card. If you have a playable card you must play it; you can only pass when nothing fits.
            • A PRIME can be played any time (if its slot is free).
            • A composite can be played only when all its prime factors are on the board. For example, 12 needs 2, 2 and 3.
            • MAGIC is a wild card that counts as any prime you choose. It must go on an empty prime slot, and the real card can later go on top of it. Only when every prime slot is filled can MAGIC go on top of a card already on the board, where it counts as one more of that prime. So MAGIC can always be played.
            • SKIP makes a player of your choice miss their next turn. They still have to draw a card from the tray.
          5. Your hand shows cards in the order they arrived. New cards go on the right. Drag a card to move it (on a phone, press and hold, then drag), or select it and use the ← → keys.
          6. Always draw. Whether you play or pass, you must pick a card from the tray at the end of your turn (while the tray has cards).
          7. Winning. Once the tray is empty, the first player to get rid of all their cards wins.
          8. Series. The host chooses how many games the same players will play. Scores add up across the games. The lowest total wins the series, and the highest total comes last.
          9. Scoring. Everyone else adds up the numbers left in their hand (Magic & Skip = 0). Lower is better. Scores add up across a series of games.

          Players can’t leave during a game. If someone drops out, the host can remove them and their unplayed cards go back into the tray.
